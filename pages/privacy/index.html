Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Reels Autoposter ("Service") handles information when publishing promotional short videos for a Telegram channel through TikTok and Instagram.

1. Data we process

  • Telegram user IDs of authorized operators
  • Videos and captions submitted for publishing (including promo creatives for the Telegram channel)
  • OAuth tokens and account identifiers for connected TikTok/Instagram accounts
  • Technical logs needed to run publishing jobs

2. How we use data

Data is used only to process media, publish posts you request, and show job status. We do not sell personal data.

3. Sharing

Content and tokens are shared with TikTok and Meta only as required by their official APIs to perform actions you authorize.

4. Storage

Data is stored on the operator-controlled server used to run the Service. Videos may be temporarily stored for processing and upload.

5. Retention

Media and logs may be deleted periodically after publishing. You can request deletion of stored tokens and media by contacting the operator.

6. Your choices

Disconnect platform access in TikTok/Meta settings. Stop using the Telegram bot to prevent new processing.

7. Contact

Privacy requests can be sent via the Telegram bot used to operate this Service.
